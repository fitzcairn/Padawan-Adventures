

Levels are cumulative, but probabilities strongest over latest level.

Level 1:

s, a, t, p, i, n

at, it, in, an, sat, sit, pat, tap, pan, nap, pin, sip


Level 2: 

m, d, g, o, c, k

kam, dog, cat, mad, pig, dad, mat, map, dig, cat, can, pig, pot, mop, kid 


Level 3: 

e, u, r, h, b, f, l

run, bed, bug, sun, hat


Level 4: 

j, v, w, x, y, z, g

jam, vet, web, fox, zip, padawan, jump, vest, quit, quiz



Level 5: Beginning blends

st, sn, sp, fl, pl, sl, tr, fr, br

stop, snap, spot, flag, plum, slip, tree, frog, tris, brush, crab


Level 6: Ending blends

nd, mp, st, nt

sand, pond, jump, camp, fast, nest, tent, ant


Level 7: Twin consonant endings

ff, ll, ss, zz

puff, hill, kiss, buzz


Level 8: Vowel teams

ai, ay, ee, ea, oa, ow, ugh

rain, train, play, day, see, tree, sea, read, boat, coat, snow, blow, night, high



Level 9: Magic E

a_e, i_e, o_e, u_e

tape, cake, name, pipe, bike, time, note, bone, home, cube, tube, huge

a_e: tap becomes tape (cake, name)

i_e: pip becomes pipe (bike, time)

o_e: not becomes note (bone, home)

u_e: cub becomes cube (tube, huge)
